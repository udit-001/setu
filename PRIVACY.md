# Privacy Policy for Setu

**Effective Date: September 2026**

Setu ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

## 1. The Short Version

Setu is fully offline. We collect **nothing** — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no accounts. Nothing you say, transcribe, or translate ever leaves your device.

## 2. Audio Data and Microphone Access

Setu requires access to your device's microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`) to provide real-time speech-to-text functionality.

- All audio processing and speech recognition is performed **locally on your device**.
- Your voice recordings are **never** transmitted over the internet or saved to permanent storage.
- The transcribed text and translations are processed solely on your device using local AI models and are not collected by us or any third parties.

## 3. Network Access

The `INTERNET` permission is used for one purpose only: downloading the AI models (speech models per language, and the translation model on first launch). Model files are fetched from GitHub and Hugging Face. No audio, text, or usage data is transmitted.

## 4. Data Security

Because all processing happens on your device, your data is inherently secure. Your speech and translated text never leave your smartphone.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates to this policy will be reflected in this document and on our repository.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by opening an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Uktam-ai/setu).
